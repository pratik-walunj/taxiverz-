## How long we keep your details  *(owner decision 2026-09-28 — for owner review)*

When you ask for a fare, book a trip or send an enquiry, we keep the details you give us (name, phone number, email if given, trip details and the fare we quoted) for **24 months after your last contact with us**. After that we delete them.

"Last contact" means the last time you booked, enquired or otherwise got in touch with us. If you contact us again within the 24 months, the period starts again from that date.

You can ask us to delete your details sooner. *(Grievance contact and how to ask: still to be decided — OWNER_TODO H1.)*
