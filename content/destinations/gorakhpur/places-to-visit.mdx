Gorakhpur is usually a stop on the way somewhere else — to Kushinagar, to Lumbini, to Kathmandu — but it is worth a day of its own. The city grew up around the Gorakhnath Math, gave India the Gita Press, and has a large lake at its centre that the evening crowds make their own. Here is what to see, and how to fit it into a day.

## Gorakhnath Temple

The Gorakhnath Temple is the heart of the city and its name. It is the seat of the Nath tradition of yogis, dedicated to Guru Gorakhnath, and the complex is large, clean and busy from early morning. Visit at dawn for the aarti and the quiet, or in the evening when families come after work. In mid-January, at Makar Sankranti, the Khichdi Mela fills the temple and the streets around it with pilgrims offering khichdi — the biggest event of the city's year. Expect heavy traffic near the temple on that day.

## Gita Press

The Gita Press, founded in 1923, has printed Hindu scriptures — the Bhagavad Gita, the Ramcharitmanas, the Puranas — in huge numbers and at low prices for a century, along with the monthly magazine _Kalyan_. The building's gate is a landmark in itself. Inside, the Leela Chitra Mandir displays paintings of scenes from the epics, and the shop sells the Press's books. Allow an hour.

## Ramgarh Tal

Ramgarh Tal is a large lake within the city, and its lakefront has become Gorakhpur's favourite place to spend an evening. There is boating at Nauka Vihar, a long promenade, food stalls, and sunsets over the water. The Veer Bahadur Singh Planetarium stands in the Taramandal area nearby, and makes a good stop with children.

## The zoo and Kusmhi forest

The Shaheed Ashfaq Ullah Khan Zoological Park, opened in 2021, is the city's zoo, set in green grounds with enclosures for big cats and native animals. On the edge of the city, the Kusmhi forest holds the Budhiya Mai temple, a popular shrine reached through the trees, and the Vinod Van picnic area.

## Vishnu Temple and Arogya Mandir

The Vishnu Temple houses a black-stone image of Vishnu dating to around the twelfth century, a quieter stop for those interested in the city's older past. Arogya Mandir is a long-running naturopathy centre, known to people who come to Gorakhpur for its treatments.

## Golghar and the markets

For shopping and street food, Golghar is the busy commercial centre, with the Hindi Bazar and Reti Chowk areas nearby for everyday goods, fabrics and wedding shopping. Parking is tight, which is where a car with a driver helps: you step out, the car waits.
