Kushinagar can be visited all year, and pilgrims do come in every season. But the weather in this part of eastern Uttar Pradesh changes a great deal between winter, summer and the monsoon, and it shapes how comfortable a visit will be.

## October to March: the best months

From October to March the days are dry and pleasant, and the sites — mostly open-air, with walking between temples, stupas and ruins — are easy to enjoy. October and November are warm by day and cool in the evening; the fields around the town are green after the rains. December and January are the coldest months, with chilly mornings and nights, and this is also the peak season for international pilgrims, especially from Thailand, Sri Lanka and Myanmar, who travel in groups on the Buddhist circuit. February and March are mild and bright.

The one thing to plan around in winter is fog. In December and January, dense fog often settles over the plains in the early morning and late evening, and the highways slow down badly. If you are driving from Gorakhpur or further, leave after the fog lifts, and avoid driving back late at night.

## April to June: hot

From April the heat builds, and May and June are very hot, with strong sun and dry, dusty winds. Visits are still possible — start early in the morning, rest indoors in the middle of the day, and return to the sites in the late afternoon. Carry water, a hat and sunscreen, and remember that shoes are removed in many temples, so the stone floors can get hot.

Buddha Purnima, the full-moon day that marks the Buddha's birth, enlightenment and passing, usually falls in April or May. It is the most important festival in Kushinagar, with prayers, processions and many visitors; if you want to see it, book early and expect crowds.

## July to September: the monsoon

The monsoon brings heavy showers, high humidity and green, lush surroundings. Rain can be intense for an hour and then clear. Roads can be waterlogged after heavy rain, and some low-lying areas flood in a bad year, so keep plans flexible and check conditions before a long drive. The upside is fewer visitors and a peaceful atmosphere at the sites.

## Our suggestion

For a first visit, choose any time between late October and early March, avoid the foggiest early mornings on the road, and allow a full day to see the sites without hurrying. If you are combining Kushinagar with [Lumbini](/destinations/lumbini/best-time-to-visit/), the same months suit both.
