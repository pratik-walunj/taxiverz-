Lumbini lies in the Terai, the flat, low-lying plains of southern Nepal, and its weather is much like that of eastern Uttar Pradesh across the border: pleasant in winter, very hot before the monsoon, and wet from June to September. The sites are almost all outdoors and spread across a wide area, so the weather matters.

## October to March: the best months

The months from October to March are the best time to visit. After the monsoon, October and November bring clear skies, green fields and comfortable days — good weather for walking through the Sacred Garden and between the monasteries. December and January are cool, with cold mornings and nights; bring a warm layer. February and March are bright and mild.

This is also the main pilgrimage season, when groups from Thailand, Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Vietnam, Korea and elsewhere travel the Buddhist circuit. The Maya Devi Temple can have a queue in the middle of the day, so go early.

In December and January, dense fog can settle over the Terai and the Indian plains in the early morning and evening. It slows road travel on both sides of the border; plan to drive in the middle of the day.

## April to June: hot

From April the heat rises, and May and June are very hot, with temperatures often around forty degrees. With little shade between the monastic zone's temples, visits in the middle of the day are hard work. Start at dawn, rest in the afternoon, and return in the late afternoon.

Buddha Jayanti — Buddha Purnima — the full-moon day celebrating the Buddha's birth, falls in April or May. It is the most important day of the year in Lumbini, with prayers, processions and many visitors, especially at the Maya Devi Temple. It is a moving time to be there, if you are ready for the heat and the crowds.

## June to September: the monsoon

The monsoon brings heavy rain, humidity and flooded fields around the site. Paths can be muddy and the monasteries quieter. Rain often comes in bursts, so visits are possible with flexible plans, but long drives on waterlogged roads can take longer than expected.

## Our suggestion

For a first visit, go between late October and early March, start early in the day, and allow most of a day for the temple, the garden and the monasteries. The same months suit [Kushinagar](/destinations/kushinagar/best-time-to-visit/), which many pilgrims visit on the same trip.
