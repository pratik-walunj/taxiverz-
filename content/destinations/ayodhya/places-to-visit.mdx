Ayodhya sits on the southern bank of the Saryu and is revered as the birthplace of Lord Ram. Since the Ram Mandir was consecrated on 22 January 2024, the town has changed quickly — new roads, a new station building and an airport — but the old lanes, the ghats and the hundreds of smaller temples are still there. Here is what to see, and how to plan the day.

## Ram Mandir

The Ram Janmabhoomi temple is the reason most people come. Built in the Nagara style from pink sandstone, it stands on the site believed to be Ram's birthplace, with the idol of Ram Lalla in the sanctum. Queues can be long, especially on weekends, festivals and holidays, so arrive early. Phones, bags and most belongings are not allowed inside the temple complex; there are facilities to deposit them near the entrance. Rules and timings change from time to time, so check the current ones before you go.

## Hanuman Garhi

Hanuman Garhi is a fortress-like temple to Hanuman on a small hill in the old town, reached by a flight of steps. By tradition, devotees visit Hanuman Garhi before going on to the Ram temple. The lanes around it are crowded, with sweet shops selling laddoos to offer, and parking is some way off — a car can drop you nearby and wait.

## Kanak Bhawan

Kanak Bhawan, a short walk from Hanuman Garhi, is a temple to Ram and Sita that tradition says was given to Sita by Kaikeyi. The present building, with its large courtyard and gold-crowned idols, is one of the most beautiful in the town, and much calmer than the main sites.

## Ram ki Paidi and the Saryu ghats

Ram ki Paidi is a long series of ghats on the Saryu, where pilgrims bathe and where the evening aarti is held. At Diwali it is the centre of Deepotsav, when the ghats are lit with lakhs of diyas. Guptar Ghat, further along the river, is where Ram is believed to have taken jal samadhi; it is quieter and good for a walk by the water.

## Other temples

Nageshwarnath Temple, near Ram ki Paidi, is an old Shiva temple traditionally said to have been founded by Kush, Ram's son. Dashrath Mahal, Treta ke Thakur and the Sita Ki Rasoi shrine are all within the old town and easy to add if you have time.

## Planning your day

A comfortable day in Ayodhya starts with Hanuman Garhi and the Ram Mandir in the morning, Kanak Bhawan and the old-town temples around midday, and Ram ki Paidi for the evening aarti. Wear comfortable shoes you can slip off, carry water, and keep cash for offerings.

Ayodhya lies west of Gorakhpur along National Highway 27, and many visitors come as a long day trip or stay overnight to see the evening aarti.
