A wedding in Gorakhpur usually needs more cars than a family expects, and in the wedding season the good ones are booked early. Here is a practical way to plan them.

## Count the journeys, not the cars

Start by listing every journey on the wedding days: guests arriving at Gorakhpur Junction or the airport, the groom's car for the baraat, family cars to the venue, the couple's car after the vidaai, and guests going back. Some cars can do several of these; others need to wait at the venue.

## The groom's car

This is the one everyone remembers. Decide early whether you want a luxury car, and whether you want it decorated. Tell the car company the timings: when the baraat leaves, how long it takes, and when the car is free again.

## Cars for family and guests

For close family, sedans and MPVs such as the Innova Crysta are comfortable and easy to park near venues. For guests, a tempo traveller or an Urbania moves a group in one go, with one driver and one set of timings. See [tempo travellers in Gorakhpur](/tempo-traveller/gorakhpur/).

## When to book

As early as you can. Popular wedding dates are the same for every family, and cars, drivers and decoration are booked in order.

## What to tell your car company

- The dates and the timings for each journey
- Pickup and drop points, including stations and hotels
- The number of guests in each group, and their luggage
- Anything special: decoration, a particular colour, a driver who can wait late
