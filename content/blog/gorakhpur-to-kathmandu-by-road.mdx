Kathmandu is one of the most common long trips from Gorakhpur, for pilgrims going to Pashupatinath, families visiting relatives and tourists starting a Nepal holiday. Here is what to expect on the road.

## The crossing

From Gorakhpur, the road runs north to the border at Sonauli, opposite Belahiya and Bhairahawa in Nepal. Another crossing, at Raxaul–Birgunj in Bihar, is closer to Kathmandu but further from Gorakhpur. We confirm the crossing for your trip when you book.

## The route through the hills

Beyond the border, the road goes to Butwal and then climbs into the hills towards Kathmandu. Much of the drive is on mountain roads with bends, climbs and slow stretches behind trucks. It is best driven in daylight, with breaks.

## Documents

Indian citizens don't need a visa for Nepal. Carry a valid passport or Voter ID card. Call or WhatsApp us for the full document checklist before you travel.

## How long to allow

Plan for a full day on the road and an early start. Don't plan tight connections on arrival.
