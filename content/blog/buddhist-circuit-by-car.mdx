The Buddha's life can be traced across a small corner of India and Nepal. He was born at Lumbini, found enlightenment at Bodh Gaya, gave his first sermon at Sarnath and passed away at Kushinagar. All four lie within reach of Gorakhpur by road, which makes it a natural base for the Buddhist circuit.

## The four great sites

- **Lumbini**, in southern Nepal: the birthplace, with the Maya Devi Temple and the Ashoka Pillar. See [places to visit in Lumbini](/destinations/lumbini/places-to-visit/).
- **Kushinagar**, east of Gorakhpur: the place of the mahaparinirvana, with the great reclining Buddha. See [places to visit in Kushinagar](/destinations/kushinagar/places-to-visit/).
- **Sarnath**, just outside Varanasi: the deer park where the Buddha first taught, with the Dhamek Stupa. See [places to visit in Varanasi](/destinations/varanasi/places-to-visit/).
- **Bodh Gaya**, in Bihar: the Mahabodhi Temple and the Bodhi tree, where the Buddha attained enlightenment.

## An order that works

Starting from Gorakhpur, many pilgrims go north to Lumbini first, return to Kushinagar, then head south-west to Varanasi and Sarnath, and finish at Bodh Gaya — or do the circuit the other way round if they arrive at Gaya.

## How long to allow

Each site deserves at least half a day, and Lumbini and Bodh Gaya a full day each. With the long drives between them, most people allow a week for all four without rushing.

## When to go

October to March is the pilgrimage season, with comfortable weather and the largest groups from abroad. See [the best time to visit Lumbini](/destinations/lumbini/best-time-to-visit/) and [the best time to visit Kushinagar](/destinations/kushinagar/best-time-to-visit/).

## Travelling by car

A car and driver for the whole circuit lets you set your own pace, stop where you like and keep your luggage with you between sites. Choose an MPV or a tempo traveller for a family or a group. See [outstation cabs from Gorakhpur](/outstation-cabs/gorakhpur/).
